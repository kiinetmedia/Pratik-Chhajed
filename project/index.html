When to come in

Three reasons most people walk in.

Most evenings, patients arrive with one of these three concerns. If yours doesn't fit neatly, that's fine — internal medicine is the broad doorway into adult care, and a 20-minute consultation usually settles it.

Acute

Something started this week.

A fever that hasn't broken in 72 hours. A headache that's different from your usual. Chest tightness on a flight of stairs. Breathing that feels heavier than it should. A stomach pain that keeps returning to the same spot.

Worth a same-evening slot. Most acute cases need one consultation and, if needed, a basic blood panel.

Book a slot tonight →
Chronic

Something to manage long-term.

A new diabetes or BP diagnosis you want explained properly. A thyroid or cholesterol report that needs translating into a plan. An older prescription that hasn't been reviewed in three years and probably should be.

A relationship, not a transaction. Most patients return every 3–6 months, with WhatsApp follow-ups in between.

Start with a baseline visit →
Second opinion

Something you want reviewed.

A diagnosis that didn't quite sit right. A test panel you suspect was over-prescribed. A surgery recommendation you want sense-checked before saying yes. Bring whatever reports you have — even photos on a phone are fine.

You'll leave with a written summary you can take to any other doctor. No pressure to switch practices.

Bring your reports in →

Not sure if your concern fits any of these? It probably does — and if it doesn't, we'll tell you who to see instead. Just call the clinic.

About Dr. Pratik

An evening practice in Hiranandani Estate.

Nestled in the heart of Thane, Dr. Pratik Chhajed, MD Medicine, runs an unhurried internal medicine practice at Doctors Planet NX, adjacent to Hiranandani Hospital. The clinic is a familiar evening stop for families across Hiranandani Estate, Majiwada, Manpada, and the Param Hospital and Bhanushali Hospital neighbourhoods of Thane West.

With a decade of hospital-trained experience and a genuine commitment to patient well-being, Dr. Chhajed expertly addresses the conditions that quietly shape adult life in this city — diabetes, hypertension, heart conditions, neurological concerns, kidney health, gut and liver disorders, thyroid issues, and joint problems. The aim is simple: care that feels personal, explanations that feel honest, and a treatment plan you actually understand before you leave the room.

Most patients arrive worried, leave clearer, and stay reachable on WhatsApp afterwards. A consultation here is not a transaction — it is the start of a conversation that continues for as long as you need it to.

Frequently asked

The four we hear the most.

How do I book an appointment?
Use the booking form on this site, or call/WhatsApp the clinic directly. We confirm every booking within 30 minutes during clinic hours.
Do you accept walk-ins?
Yes, but booked patients are seen first. Evening walk-ins typically wait 20–40 minutes; booking is faster.
What should I bring to the consultation?
Any previous reports, current medications (or photos of the strips), and a one-line note of your main concern. That's it.
What if I have an emergency?
Please don't wait for the clinic to open. Call 108 or go directly to Hiranandani Hospital, which is next door.
